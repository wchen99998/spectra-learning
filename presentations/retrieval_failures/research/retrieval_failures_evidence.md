## Reproduction checks

- The recomputed ensemble reproduces the reported results exactly: validation Hit@1 0.4826, test 0.47334 (8,310 of 17,556).
- Candidate order in the score dumps equals the supplied pool order (`run_retrieval_jax.py`: `candidates = pool[truth]`), so candidate index → SMILES is exact; the truth is always index 0.
- Clustering unit: InChIKey14 of the query (2,998 test and 1,911 validation clusters). Bootstrap: 1,000 resamples for stratum means; 200 for logistic refits; `numpy.random.default_rng(0)`.

## Definitions

| Quantity | Definition |
|---|---|
| Hit@1 | Truth ranked first among its supplied formula pool, stable ties (no ties occur at the truth) |
| Ensemble score | Sum over 15 members of per-query z-scored candidate scores |
| Tanimoto | RDKit Morgan fingerprint, radius 2, 2,048 bits |
| Train similarity | Maximum Tanimoto of the truth to any unique train-fold SMILES |
| Max decoy similarity | Maximum Tanimoto of the truth to any other candidate in its pool |
| Explained intensity | Share of observed intensity within 0.01 Da of a GLACIER fragment ion: fragment mass + adduct (and proton for [M+Na]⁺), −2..+2 H shifts |
| Near-isomer miss | Tanimoto(wrong top-1, truth) ≥ 0.7 |
| Uninformative spectrum | ≤ 5 peaks |
| Fragment-indistinguishable miss | Wrong top-1 explains ≥ the truth's intensity (40% strictly more, 38% ties; 14% both zero) |
| Counterfactual Hit@1 | Overall Hit@1 if a stratum's Hit@1 equalled that of all other spectra |

## Qualifications

- Test analysis is post hoc and diagnostic; it selects nothing. The strata replicate on validation, except that the 600–700 Da validation bin (104 molecules) is above average and the QTOF gap is smaller on validation (41.8% vs 48.3%).
- Strata overlap; counterfactuals are arithmetic, not predictions.
- Fragment evidence uses the stored 128 GLACIER proposals and the scorer's kernel; it is a mass-matching diagnostic, not the learned model's internal score.
- Fragment-generator bounds use 600 validation queries; conditioned proposals cover the 215 of them with [M+Na]⁺ or QTOF acquisition.
- Levers on slide 15 are proposals, not results.
